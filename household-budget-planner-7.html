A few things that help

Save before it disappears. If you can, move even $5–$10 into savings right after income arrives, before it's absorbed into spending.
Build a small buffer first. A few hundred dollars set aside can prevent a surprise bill from becoming a missed rent payment.
Check what you may qualify for. Programs like SNAP, LIHEAP (energy assistance), WIC, and the Earned Income Tax Credit exist to help — they're worth checking regardless of past eligibility.
List bills by due date, not just amount. Knowing what's due when helps you avoid late fees more than knowing totals alone.
Round numbers are fine. This planner works even with rough estimates — refine them as real numbers come in.
Revisit monthly. Income and bills shift. A five-minute check-in each payday keeps this useful.
